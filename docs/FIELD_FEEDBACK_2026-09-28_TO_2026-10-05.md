- **Audit** — compare the report with the current foundation build.

- **Planned enhancement** — useful request whose scope belongs to a later reviewed
  change rather than a defect correction.
- **Partially complete** — verified portions are corrected; a separately reviewed
  portion remains open.
- **Complete** — corrected and verified in the current foundation build.
